# Play Store listing assets — Fitrater

Phone set rebuilt **2026-08-03**; the tablet sets are still from the **2026-08-01**
pass. Together these supersede the six loose `N_*.png` files in this folder, three
of which were unusable (copyrighted press photo in two, a screen-recorder bubble in
the third).

## What to upload where

| Play Console field | Folder / file | Size | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone screenshots | `phone/` | 1440x2560 (9:16) | 8 |
| 7-inch tablet screenshots | `tab7/` | 1600x2560 (5:8) | 8 |
| 10-inch tablet screenshots | `tab10/` | 1800x2880 (5:8) | 8 |
| Feature graphic | `../feature-graphic-1024x500-v2.jpg` | 1024x500 | 1 |

Every side is between 320 px and 3840 px, no side is more than twice the other,
and the largest file is well under the 8 MB cap. Eight phone shots at >=1080 px in
9:16 clears the **>=4 screenshots** bar for store-promotion eligibility, which the
old three-screenshot listing did not.

Upload order is filename order. **Play accepts at most 8 screenshots per device
type** — that is the budget the panel list below is fighting over.

> **The tablet sets no longer match the phone set.** `tab7/` and `tab10/` still
> carry the 2026-08-01 panels, which include Score breakdown and You but neither
> Try-on nor Studio. Play allows a different set per device type, so this is
> uploadable as-is, but re-running the tablets is worth doing. To do it, put
> `tab7`/`tab10` back into `SPECS` in `harness/compose_store2.py` and back into the
> AVD loop in `harness/recapture.sh`.

## `raw/` — unframed captures

`raw/{phone,tab7,tab10}/` holds every screen captured per device, straight off the
device, no caption and no phone frame. That is the folder to play with if you want
to reframe anything yourself. The composed sets use 8 of them.

## Device frame

`harness/phone3d.py` renders the 3D phone with no external assets: the flat body
(bezel, rounded screen, punch-hole, glare) is built first, then treated as a slab
in 3D, rotated and projected. The side wall follows the body's real rounded
silhouette and is shaded per-segment by its outward normal — cutting straight
corner-to-corner quads instead made the edge read as a flat card behind the phone.
Contact shadow underneath.

## Where the photos came from

`../photos/` holds AI-generated photos (Gemini) in two batches:

- **Looks** — eight full-body street shots and mirror selfies, four looks each of
  two people (`man_*.jpg`, `woman_*.jpg`).
- **Garments** — eight flat-lays used as closet pieces (`piece_*.jpg`), one worn
  shot used as the try-on result (`tryon_*.jpg`), and seven worn singles kept as
  spares (`spare_*.jpg`).

Before use the sparkle watermark was removed and the C2PA / SynthID provenance
manifest stripped. `finalize3.py` runs the garment batch end to end; `unblend.py`,
`dewatermark.py` and `alpha_fit.py` are the shared primitives.

The sparkle is a fixed alpha composite of white at a constant corner offset
(W-117, H-121), so one solved alpha map serves a whole batch. Two corrections came
out of the garment batch:

- **The watermark colour is white (C=255), not ~230.** A grid search on
  reconstruction residual bottoms out near 230, but that minimum is an artefact —
  the residual is dominated by texture the Laplace fill misses, and the curve is
  nearly flat across the whole range. The snow shot settles it: its background sits
  at 233 and the sparkle *brightens* it to 240, which is only possible if C > 233.
- **`unblend.py`'s `gaussian_filter(A, 0.8)` is too much.** The star's anti-aliased
  rim is 1-2 px; blurring it lowers and spreads the rim alpha, which leaves a
  bright outline on dark wood even when the star's interior is already perfect.
  `sigma=0.3` with a `0.004` cutoff (not `0.02`) removes it cleanly. With 16 images
  x 3 channels per pixel the solve does not need the smoothing.

A per-image alpha scale is measured on the star core, where the Laplace baseline is
smoothest. Five photos get it forced to 1.0 because their fill crosses a hard edge
(a bench, a leg, a wall corner) and the measurement comes back as garbage — the
same failure mode recorded for the earlier batch.

Also tried and rejected: a linear-light composite model (worse — r=-0.29 and an
unphysical C>1), and growing the two widest flat-lays to a square by replicating
their edge rows (smears plank grain into vertical streaks, and duplicated the white
backdrop wedges in the jeans shot). Those two are cropped instead; the jeans crop
is biased left to keep the waistband, fly and pockets, which is what makes them
read as jeans in a 160dp tile.

Two of the eighteen garment source files were dropped: they are Gemini contact-sheet
collages rather than product shots, and carry fabricated brand marks ("U.B.",
"URBAN BOSKO").

**SynthID is still in the pixels.** It was not touched and cannot be removed with
these tools. These are synthetic people and synthetic garments, not real users,
customers or products.

## How the screens were populated

No sign-in, no backend call, no spent credit. A local harness (`harness/`) seeds
`Repo` with four fixed looks and eight closet pieces, opens the Pro and credit
gates, and preloads a dedicated photo into each feature screen, so the **real UI**
renders with representative data. It was reverted out of the working tree after
capture.

To re-capture:

```bash
cd android
mkdir -p app/src/main/java/com/fitrater/app/data/demo
cp store/screenshots/harness/Demo.kt app/src/main/java/com/fitrater/app/data/demo/
mkdir -p app/src/main/assets/demo
cp store/photos/man_*.jpg store/photos/woman_street_camel_coat.jpg \
   store/photos/piece_*.jpg store/photos/tryon_charcoal_overcoat.jpg \
   app/src/main/assets/demo/
git apply store/screenshots/harness/demo-harness.patch
source env.sh && ./gradlew :app:assembleDebug
store/screenshots/harness/recapture.sh   # boots the AVD, walks the app -> raw/phone/
python3 store/screenshots/harness/compose_store2.py    # raw/ -> phone/
git checkout -- app/src/main/java/com/fitrater/app/
rm -rf app/src/main/java/com/fitrater/app/data/demo app/src/main/assets
```

Run the `mkdir` from `android/`, not from inside the package directory. From the
wrong cwd it creates a nested `app/src/main/...` under the package and the APK
silently ships **zero** assets. Check with:

```bash
unzip -l app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk | grep -c assets/demo   # expect 14
```

`demo-harness.patch` touches nine files — `MainActivity.kt`, `Repo.kt`,
`HemService.kt`, `CompareService.kt`, `RcBilling.kt`, `RoastScreen.kt`,
`VersusScreen.kt`, `DecodeScreen.kt` and `TryOnScreen.kt` — all reverted by the
`git checkout` above.

`recapture.sh` drives `fit_phone` only (the portrait `fit_tab7` / `fit_tab10` AVDs
still exist from the previous pass; the pre-existing `fit_tablet` is landscape).
`clean_ui.sh` pins the clock to 9:41 and puts SysUI in demo mode so no debug or adb
icons appear in the status bar — its network line needs `-e fully true` or every
capture ships a wifi glyph with the "!" no-internet badge.

`uidriver.py` taps by **visible text**, resolved from a `uiautomator` dump, and
scrolls if the target is below the fold. Screen-fraction taps were measured on the
phone and silently hit the wrong rows on tablets, and the launcher throws an
"isn't responding" ANR under load that swallows every tap — `uidriver` clears it
before each tap and each screenshot.

Try-on is the one screen with no action to drive: the harness presets `resultUrl`,
so it opens straight on the result and the walk just shoots it.

## Two things worth knowing

**The serif never loads.** Known issue #13 says the downloadable Google Font
(Playfair Display) falls back to system sans "offline / no Play Services". It is
worse than that: it also falls back on the **google_apis_playstore** image with
Play Services installed and a working network — the app never even issues the
Playfair query (zero hits in the font provider log, which serves other font
requests fine). Real users are almost certainly seeing Roboto too, so the serif
brand identity is not currently shipping. These screenshots show what users
actually get, which is the honest thing for a listing, but the bug is worth fixing
on its own.

**Tablet layout is the phone layout stretched.** Known issue #7 — there is no
large-screen layout, so the tablet captures are wide-margin phone UI. Legitimate
and uploadable, but a real tablet layout would make them much stronger.
