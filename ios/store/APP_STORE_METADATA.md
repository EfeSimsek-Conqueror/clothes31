## Description (4000 char max)

```
Fitrater is a private style desk. Rate an outfit, build a wardrobe, try garments on your own body, and see any look staged like a magazine cover — all in one editorial workspace.

SCORE
Photograph the fit you are about to wear. Fitrater reads silhouette, fabric, palette, and occasion, then returns a numeric score with a short editorial note from Hem, a resident critic who writes like a magazine editor. No hearts, no streaks, no fluff. Just an honest read.

STUDIO
Save every piece you own. Studio holds a clean grid of your closet — coats, denim, knits, shoes — with tags for palette, silhouette, and season. Pieces are reusable: drop them into Try-on, into Cover, into a saved Look.

TRY-ON
Calibrate once with a full-length photo. From then on, any garment you upload — a Studio piece, a screenshot from a shop, a photo of a friend's coat — renders on your own body. See how the shoulder sits, how the hem lands, how the color reads against your skin, before the parcel arrives.

COMPOSE COVER
Turn a Try-on or a saved Look into a magazine spread. Pick a template — Editorial, High Fashion, Street — and Fitrater composes the shot with typography, cover lines, and a masthead. Save it to Journal, share it, or keep it as a private mood.

JOURNAL
Every score, every generated cover, every Try-on is kept in a quiet timeline. Filter by season, by score, by palette. Weekly Wrapped surfaces your highest-rated looks and the pieces that carried them.

YOU
Style DNA is a lightweight profile the app builds from what you wear and what you save. It shapes the tone of Hem's notes and the templates Cover suggests. You can edit it, reset it, or turn it off.

HEM
Hem is the resident critic. Every score arrives with a line from Hem — a sentence, sometimes two, in the voice of a magazine editor. No emojis. No exclamation. Just the read.

PRIVACY
Photos are stored in your private account. Rendering is performed by our AI vendor for the length of the request and is not retained for training. You can delete any photo, any score, and your account at any time from Settings.

PRO
Fitrater is free to try. Fitrater Pro unlocks unlimited scoring, unlimited Studio pieces, higher-resolution Try-on and Cover renders, brutal-mode critiques, and the weekly Sunday Letter from Hem. Credit packs are available for one-off Try-on and Cover renders without a subscription.

Fitrater is a workspace for the way you actually get dressed. It is not a social network. There is no feed. There are no followers. There is only your closet, your body, and a critic who will tell you the truth.
```
